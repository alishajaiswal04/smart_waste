About Smart Waste

Smart Waste is an intelligent waste management platform aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). It helps cities reduce pollution, optimize resources, and build cleaner, more sustainable communities.

📡

Real-time Monitoring

IoT-enabled bins provide live fill-level data to prevent overflow and unnecessary collection trips.

🚚

Smart Route Optimization

Rule-based and AI-assisted scheduling reduces fuel usage, emissions, and operational cost.

📊

Data-driven Decisions

Analytics dashboards help municipalities plan capacity, predict waste patterns, and improve efficiency.

Aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals

SDG 11

Sustainable Cities & Communities

Improves urban cleanliness, optimizes waste collection, and supports smart city infrastructure.

SDG 12

SDG 13
